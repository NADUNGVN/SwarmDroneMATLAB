## Seed correction exposed by R2.5

The registry configurations are constructed with seed `27022001`.  The frozen
N=5 witness scenario in the R2 driver was constructed with seed `27020001`.
The practical reference directions use independent deterministic
`RandStream` seeds `27022001 + actionIndex`.  No stochastic scenario/channel
draw enters the held-memory witness replay, so the discrepancy does not alter
the values, but the historical R2 `summary.seed=27022001` is incomplete
metadata and must not be cited as a single seed covering both components.
