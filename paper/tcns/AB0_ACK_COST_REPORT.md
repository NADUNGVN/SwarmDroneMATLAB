## C0/C1/C2 accounting

- C0 is the frozen O1 cost with zero ACK.
- C1 charges one ACK for every actually accepted O1 DATA delivery.
- C2 reproduces the implemented causal protocol's cumulative ACK generation.
  `simSwarmAoICausal` invokes DATA delivery before scheduling and again after
  scheduling for zero-delay packets. C2 therefore permits one ACK per link in
  each of these two delivery phases. It groups accepted actions by delivery
  tick, phase, receiver, sender and payload class.

C1 and C2 are equal in every one of the 350 O1 runs. Under these trajectories,
no delivery phase accepts multiple O1 generations for the same directed
payload link, so cumulative aggregation saves no ACK transmissions. This is a
result of the frozen action/arrival history, not an imposed equality.

Across the 14 O1 operating points and five seeds, C2 adds a mean 1.08--1.99
Hz/channel depending on scenario. Median relative cost increases are 20.0%
(S2), 14.1% (S3), 18.0% (S4), 18.7% (S5), and 20.1% (S6).
