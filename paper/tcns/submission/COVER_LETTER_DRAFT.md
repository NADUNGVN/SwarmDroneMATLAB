Decidable? Information-Structure Limits in Distributed Formation Control,” by
[VERIFIED AUTHOR LIST], for publication in the IEEE Transactions on Control of

The manuscript studies a prerequisite for control-aware communication:
whether the conditional fixed-response closed-loop benefit used to decide a
transmission is identifiable under the distributed information structure, and
quantifies the local decision loss and supplementary linear information
required when it is not.

The paper uses established functional-observability geometry without claiming
the generic row-space criterion as new. Its contribution is the
control-communication synthesis: an exact finite-horizon communication-action
functional, compatible-set minimax value and binary-decision regret, the
minimum instantaneous linear-statistic dimension for multiple actions,
coalition ownership, implementation-faithful reachable formation witnesses,
and a packet-frequency acquisition-cost screen.
