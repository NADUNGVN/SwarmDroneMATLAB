\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A fixed causal ACK-assisted AoI policy can adapt communication effort within a
mission without a network-regime label. Dual confirmed/transmitted memory and
the innovation/refresh split explain the v1--v3 improvement. The 50-seed
holdout supports Stressed accuracy against P10 but shows that DATA savings
reverse under ACK-inclusive accounting. EXP11 supports within-run adaptivity
and its preregistered P20 cost contrast, while P12.5 and broadcast accounting
rule out universal dominance. Together with the 6-DOF and boundary evidence,
the results support a mechanism of causal adaptivity with explicit operating
limits, not a general reduction in total radio traffic, hardware validation,
universal robustness, or stability claim.
