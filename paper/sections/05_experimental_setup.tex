\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

The scientific simulation and result files were frozen before manuscript
editing. EXP07 defined the design ablations; EXP10 preregistered the final
Causal-v3 configuration and evaluated 50 unseen seeds in Clean, Moderate, and
Stressed regimes. Stochastic inputs are pre-drawn by link/timestep or physical
time, so silence by one method does not shift another method's realization.
Reported differences therefore use matched seeds and Student-$t$ 95\%
intervals of the seed-level paired differences. P$k$ denotes a fixed
$k$-Hz-per-channel scheduler; EXP11 uses P5, P10, P12.5, P20, and P25.
State-event uses the hard-innovation branch with max silence but no freshness
term. The design study adds causal ablations and an ideal-feedback reference;
EXP11 adds a regime-aware oracle-periodic reference. The ideal policies are
an ideal-feedback or oracle-information efficiency reference, not a bound on
attainable accuracy or performance.

At the nominal point, $N=5$, $\Delta t=0.02$ s, mission duration is 30 s,
and evaluation begins at 8 s. The fixed trigger uses
$\epsilon_p=0.05$ m, $\epsilon_v=0.10$ m/s, $\bar A=0.12$ s,
$\tau_{\min}=0.02$ s, $\tau_r=0.10$ s, $\tau_{\max}=0.50$ s,
$s_0=0.50$, $s_{\min}=0.20$, and $\rho=1$.

Primary accuracy is formation RMSE. Communication is reported under two
conventions that are never pooled: EXP07 reports DATA/ACK rates
\textbf{per channel}; EXP08 and later report rates as \textbf{swarm totals}.
The later graph convention also removes in-links to the leader, so absolute
traffic is comparable within each table, not between EXP07 and EXP08+.
DATA-only cost is distinct from
$\mathrm{Total}_w=\mathrm{DATA}+w\,\mathrm{ACK}$, and broadcast accounting
is the idealized proxy in \eqref{eq:costbcast}, not an actual broadcast-medium
simulation.
The preregistered EXP11 comparison uses $w=0.25$.

EXP11 is supplemental scientific evidence outside the
\texttt{simulation-v1.0} release boundary. It places one fixed policy in a
single mission with five segments and four adjacent transitions, again over
50 matched seeds. No regime label is supplied. All headline statistics,
including adjacent rate-difference CIs, the P20 comparison, broadcast reversal,
and ideal-periodic reference, are generated from the persisted frozen EXP11
directory rather than transcribed into the manuscript.

The final validation spans \MetricTotalCells{} point-scenario cells covering
both plants, larger swarms, ACK impairment, faults, mismatch, and estimator
noise. Fault-safety ratios retain connected-graph or matched-no-fault
eligibility denominators rather than counting ineligible seeds as successes.
The complete parameter manifest, seed list, traffic definitions,
preregistered hypotheses, and result-path provenance remain in the
reproducibility artifact. The policy configuration is identical across
network cells; this paper runs no parameter search on the holdout data.
