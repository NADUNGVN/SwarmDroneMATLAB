## Why each rule exists

**A1 — every numeric headline is a generated macro**

A number typed into prose cannot be checked against the frozen data and will silently go stale when a dataset is regenerated.

**A2 — every metric macro used in the text is defined**

An undefined macro is a LaTeX build failure, or worse, an empty gap.

**A3 — generated macro names are letters-only**

LaTeX rejects digits and underscores in a command name.

**A4 — every referenced figure and table file exists**

A missing artefact becomes a blank box that a reader reads as an omission.

**A5 — no rejected claim appears as supported**

Seven claims were pre-registered, tested and rejected. A phrasing that asserts one of them turns a recorded negative result into a false positive.

**A6 — no unqualified communication-saving claim**

DATA falls against P20 by 16.73 Hz while the ACK-inclusive total rises by 10.67 Hz, both intervals excluding zero. An unqualified saving claim is contradicted by the frozen data.

**A7 — ACK-impairment tolerance is qualified as saturation**

EXP07B shows identical Stressed error and rate with and without 20 %% ACK loss because the adaptive scale is pinned at its floor. Reporting flatness as robustness would invert the mechanism.

**A8 — plant-mismatch failure attributed to the controller**

The B7 degradation is a steady offset from a proportional loop with no integral term, identical across all four methods. Calling it a communication failure would misattribute it.

**A9 — claim ledger has all three groups and preserves rejections**

The ledger is what a reader checks a claim against. A rejection missing from it is a rejection that has quietly disappeared.

**A10 — frozen simulation source untouched on this branch**

A figure or table that required editing a simulator is not a statement about the frozen campaign.

**A11 — both rate normalisations are labelled**

EXP07 reports per-channel Hz (P10 = 10.00) and EXP08 onward report swarm totals (P10 = 99.67 at N=5), on different channel counts. An unlabelled rate invites a false comparison.

**M1 — no fabricated citation**

A plausible-looking reference is worse than an empty section: a reader cannot tell it from a real one.

**M2 — figure content matches its caption claim**

No mechanical check can confirm a plot shows what its caption says.

## Scope

This audit checks *consistency between the manuscript and the
frozen result set*. It does not check that the results are
correct — that is what the pre-registered gates, the holdout
validation and the reproduction checks of `simulation-v1.0` do.

Rules marked MANUAL cannot be expressed mechanically and are
listed so that they are not mistaken for having passed.
