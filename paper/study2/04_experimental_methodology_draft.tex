\section{Experimental Methodology}
\label{sec:s2-methodology}

\subsection{Simulation and channel contract}

The primary system uses $N=5$, ring-2 information flow, two leader pins,
6-DOF followers, a 20-ms outer control step, a 2-ms inner step, a 12-s mission,
and evaluation after 8 s. Larger $N=10$ and $N=20$ cells use the same
communication/controller interface with double-integrator followers.

The abstract PHY uses 1-ms slots and 250 kbit/s, with 96-byte DATA, 16-byte ACK
base, and 8 bytes per cumulative ACK entry. Bare DATA spans four slots. Queues
hold four frames, sender history holds 32 generations, collision retries are
limited to two, and the ACK deadline is 20 ms. Primary CSMA has one complete
carrier-sense/interference domain; mandatory boundaries include ALOHA,
background occupancy, hidden terminals, reverse asymmetry, estimator noise,
and larger/sparser graphs.

DATA and standalone ACK links have independent, stationary two-state
Gilbert--Elliott processes on an absolute slot grid. In the Moderate cell,
good/bad loss probabilities are 0.03/0.70 with transition probabilities
0.004/0.036, yielding mean loss 0.097. Every method in a seed/cell receives
the same pre-drawn access, loss, background, and state traces. Silence by one
method never shifts another method's randomness.

\subsection{Baseline and development sequence}

The initial frozen matrix includes five-point frontiers for periodic
broadcast, state-event, causal AoI-only, AoCI-inspired, delayed-ACK belief, and
Causal-Broadcast. Mechanism arms compare causal unicast, standalone-only,
piggyback-only, and hybrid feedback. The primary EXP14A matrix contains 10,500
runs over 100 holdout seeds; EXP14B adds 4,800 OOD runs.

Unfavorable results trigger a new experiment identifier, not in-place tuning.
EXP14C/D use disjoint development seeds to factor adaptive feedback, a fixed
load guard, and analytical access scaling. EXP14E then freezes the selected
adaptive/no-guard/scaled design and seven comparators over 100 new seeds in
N5/N10/N20 CSMA and N5 ALOHA. EXP14F--H use further disjoint seeds for
policy-shadow, focal-decision replay, and N20 support auditing. Only after
those outcomes are closed does EXP14I freeze the MAC selector in
\eqref{eq:s2-mac-selector} and open another 100-seed block. EXP16 then uses a
disjoint 100-seed matched $2\times2$ design to test route-by-access interaction
directly. EXP17 keeps that estimand fixed and opens a third disjoint 100-seed
block over eight core load/loss/size contexts plus two descriptive network
boundaries. No EXP17 outcome is used to alter the policy.

\subsection{Outcomes and inference}

The independent unit is the parent seed. Primary continuous outcomes are
formation RMSE and offered-airtime utilization; true AoI, channel-busy union,
DATA/ACK airtime, collisions, access/confirmation delays, goodput, queueing,
attitude, saturation, control effort, and an energy proxy are secondary.
Safety is a raw binary guard based on minimum separation and divergence, not a
continuous metric.

Paired contrasts use matched seeds and report mean differences with two-sided
95\% paired-$t$ intervals. A deterministic 10,000-resample paired percentile
bootstrap is a sensitivity interval. EXP14E controls six one-sided primary
tests with Holm across three CSMA cells. EXP14I separately controls six
one-sided tests: RMSE and offered utilization in N5/N10 CSMA against fixed
adaptive feedback, and in N5 ALOHA against fixed piggyback. A cell passes only
when both adjusted tests reject in the registered direction and its candidate
safety count does not exceed the comparator. N20 is explicitly excluded from
the EXP14I confirmatory family.

EXP16 controls one six-test family: the registered route simple effect under
each access mechanism and their within-seed difference-in-differences, for
both primary outcomes. EXP17 controls all corresponding tests from its eight
core contexts in one 48-test Holm family. A core context requires all six
tests plus two observed safety guards. Its pre-hardware continuation rule
requires at least 6/8 supported contexts, at least 2/4 for each swarm size,
representation of both channel and background levels, and all core guards.
The reverse-asymmetric and hidden-terminal cells do not enter that rule.

\subsection{Focal-decision counterfactual}

Policy-level comparisons do not isolate one ACK because prior actions may have
already separated histories. EXP14G therefore records permitted adaptive
standalone decisions without changing behavior, selects at most one event per
seed without outcome access, and clones the complete network, plant, controller
and future exogenous inputs immediately before the target. The branches either
admit the target or suppress only its focal directed-link entries until
piggyback over a 0.75-s local horizon. Pre-decision state hashes, target
identity, intervention semantics, trace identity, censoring, and the airtime
identity are machine gates.

The registered minimum is 12 distinct parent trajectories per cell. N5,
N10, and ALOHA each supply 20; N20 supplies four. EXP14H adds 240 disjoint N20
pilots with the same window and minimum, and stops before replay when only
seven eligible parents are found. Outcomes are never used to relax support.

\subsection{Integrity and reproducibility}

Every long run snapshots its registry, source, seed block, and opened-design
manifest before performance is inspected. Matrix completeness, trace hashes,
causal conservatism, finite outputs, bounded memory, recipient accounting,
feedback semantics, divergence, and protocol invariants are machine gates.
They authorize analysis but are not performance criteria. The final EXP14I
run additionally requires the candidate to match its routed reference exactly
on 16 registered outcome/accounting fields for every seed and cell. EXP16 and
EXP17 additionally require matched absolute random arrays across all four
route/access arms, exact analytical access scaling, exact feedback-route
semantics, terminal physical accounting, and complete one-row-per-arm
matrices. Their frozen source and registry snapshots remain inside each result
directory.
